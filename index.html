The room

Meals & memories are made here.

↑ literally — it's painted on the wall

Gatti Anna is a pure-vegetarian South Indian cafe in Viman Nagar, Pune — an open counter with the menu board glowing above it and plates arriving on banana leaves and wooden platters.

The room does its own talking: “Meals and memories are made here” hangs by the plants, the entry steps climb with “Welcome to Gatti Anna · Happy tummy · Healthy steps”, and a pink poster by the door sets the house rule — “Tension lene ka nahi dene ka.”
